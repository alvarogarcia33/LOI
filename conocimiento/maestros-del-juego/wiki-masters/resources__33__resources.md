## Copper
One of the valuable and highly sought-after resources in Legends of Interactions . It is formed inside the orb as a result of processes that cannot be explained from a scientific point of view. The player can obtain some amount of copper by interacting with the orb of the corresponding type.
In the real world, copper is a soft, but at the same time quite durable metal, which makes it a very useful and widely used material. It was this metal that mankind learned to process one of the first, which gave a powerful impetus to the development of civilization.
The value of copper is also reflected in the game world. In Legends of Interactions , copper is required to craft various game items: batteries, scanners, etc. In addition, this resource can be used in a variety of game mechanics.
The player will definitely need copper to succeed in the Legends of Interactions universe. And for those who have strategic thinking, copper can become not just a resource, but a real platform for self-expression and the disclosure of leadership potential. By inspiring players to explore, create, and interact on a massive scale, this resource has the ability to push the boundaries of what is possible with every step they take in the Game.
