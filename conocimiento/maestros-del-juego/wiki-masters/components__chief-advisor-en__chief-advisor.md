## Chief Advisor
Chief Advisor
A special type of Advisor , designed to simplify and systematize work with other Advisors. It is provided to every beginner Master of Game.
The Chief Advisor groups digital assistants by region, allowing to easily switch between them to inspect your land holdings in any corner of the world. As a result, you do not need to spend a long time searching for your plots on the world map — just refer to the register created by the Chief Advisor. All communication between the Master of Game and his/her digital assistants will take place through the Chief Advisor.
