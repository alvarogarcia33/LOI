### Description of the object 
Orb ORB021005
Category: standard
The most productive and generous orb. An unusually powerful energy charge allows it to create huge amounts of matter, unmatched by any other orb. Quite a rare object.
When a player interacts with this object, both the player and the Master of Game receive the highest amount of resources . This is a kind of “cornucopia” in the world of Legends of Interactions .
Considering that the orb is incredibly powerful, its recharge time for further interaction is the longest — full 24 hours.
