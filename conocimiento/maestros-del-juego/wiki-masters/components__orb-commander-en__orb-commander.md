## Orb Commander
Booster Orb Commander
Type: trainable
Training duration: 3 days
A special tool designed to improve the Advisor's parameters in the Legends of Interactions game universe .
It allows the Master of Game to activate an automatic orb placement mode on his/her plots of land . Once the orbs are placed, the booster deactivates. To continue placing orbs further, the booster must be reactivated.
Automatic placement is available only for standard orbs that do not have advertising options.
This booster helps save time and quickly fill your game territory with objects.
The Advisor, using Orb Commander, independently places orbs by moving left to right across the plots, aiming to fill the available space as efficiently as possible. The Advisor chooses the orbs randomly without considering the resources they produce.
When equipped with Orb Commander, boosters like Orb Auto-Setter , Orb Master , Magic Line , and Smart Balancer can be used.
