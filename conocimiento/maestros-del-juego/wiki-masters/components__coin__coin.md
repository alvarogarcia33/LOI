## Coin
A game element that performs several important functions in the world of Legends of Interactions and ultimately shapes the entire in-game economy.
For some coins, players can purchase various items and resources , as well as upgrade items they already have. Players can earn coins as rewards for in-game achievements, through mining, or by finding them in the game world.
Coins serve as a strong incentive to explore new in-game territories.
Owning a significant number of coins and using them effectively is the key to successfully achieving in-game goals.
All coins are tokenized and have a counterpart on the NETSBO blockchain, which opens up vast opportunities for interaction and transactions involving them.
As a result, they can be used both within the Game itself and beyond—on the Antallagí Marketplace. A coin owner can freely move them from the Game to the Marketplace and back, or transfer them to other members of the gaming community. This makes it possible to pursue various asset management strategies while fully enjoying the benefits of tokenization.
