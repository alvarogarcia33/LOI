### Description of the object 
Orb ORB033072
Category: MAPS
The most powerful orb from the MAPS category. Objects from this category share resources with players and transmit information from the real world to the game world. At the same time, information in the form of MAPS is displayed on the map in the player’s scanner.
As a result, the player sees a message from the Master of Game from afar, without even interacting with the orb. The player just needs to click on the icon on the map to read the message, and if there are several players in a given location, then they can simultaneously receive the message transmitted by the orb.
This orb stands out from its counterpart in this category due to its fast recharge speed. It is as much as 12 times faster! An orb from this category that has almost reached its interaction limit can be charged with a special battery .
Thanks to its exceptional properties, this object is an effective tool for the Master of Game who plans running a serious business in the Legends of Interactions universe .
