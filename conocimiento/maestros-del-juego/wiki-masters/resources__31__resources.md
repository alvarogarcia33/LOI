## Silver
One of the important resources in Legends of Interactions . Silver in the game universe is a metal of absolute purity, which is unattainable in the real world. This is a unique substance that is formed in the orb as a result of physical processes that are not yet known to mankind.
In the real world, silver is a very valuable metal, endowed with amazing physical and chemical properties. Pure silver does not oxidize, does not darken, and does not even dissolve in aqua regia. It has a unique antibacterial effect. It conducts electricity well, which makes it indispensable in various industries.
In the world of Legends of Interactions , silver is not just a resource, but a key to an entire system of capabilities. It can help the player to reveal their potential and reach new heights in the virtual world. Those who use silver wisely will be able to make it a reliable tool for achieving their goals.
The resource can be used both in all sorts of game mechanics and for crafting various items. The player can obtain a certain amount of silver by interacting with an orb of the appropriate type.
