## Resources
Resources are special game objects that can be obtained as part of the gameplay and used to craft game items. In other words, this is what the player will have to collect and consume.
The most important function of the Masters of Game is to provide players with game resources by placing interaction objects on the game field. In other words, the Masters of Game will create a playground for players.
At the same time, the Masters of Game will have a number of privileges.
When a player gets a resource, the Master of Game will also be credited with a certain portion of this resource.
Players will not be able to send game resources from the Game to the blockchain. Players will only be able to receive game resources from the blockchain. No matter how many resources a player has collected, he/she can only consume them.
The Master of Game, unlike the player, will be able to withdraw game resources to the blockchain and dispose of them at their own discretion. Thus, the Masters of Game will have a monopoly on selling resources to players.
And players will have an ever-increasing demand for resources during the game. And, with a high degree of probability, they will not be able to meet it on their own and will prefer to purchase them from the Masters of Game.
