## High population density
Land plots in highly populated areas
The more people living in a particular area, the higher its economic potential — this rule also applies in Legends of Interactions . Land plots in densely populated regions offer Masters of Game significant opportunities for implementing profitable strategies.
Why should you focus on land plots in highly populated areas?
· high interaction potential;
Every person can become a player in Legends of Interactions. Therefore, the more people live in a particular area, the higher the chances that active players may be among them. This means that a land plot in such an area has the potential to generate high income.
· high potential for advertising;
Densely populated regions provide a unique opportunity for promoting brands and products. You can enter into agreements with real-world businesses and place advertising objects on your land within the game world. Given the large population in these areas, favorable deal terms can be expected.
· wide range of gameplay strategies;
Highly populated regions are ideal for implementing large-scale game strategies and creating efficient interaction networks.
· attractiveness for collectors and investors.
Land plots in densely populated areas, where human activity remains consistently high, also have long-term value. The likelihood of their price increasing over time makes them attractive for profitable resale in the future.
Thus, owning land plots in highly populated regions is a great way to implement profitable strategies that will lead to success in your Legends of Interactions business.
The digital assistant — the Advisor — can assist the Master of Game in selecting land plots in densely populated areas.
