## Researcher 90D
Booster Researcher 90D
Type: temporary
Duration: 90 days
A special tool designed to improve the Advisor's parameters in the Legends of Interactions game universe .
It allows the Advisor to monitor resources placed on his/her own territories and the plots of other Masters of Game within the available visibility. Once a day, the Advisor with this booster provides a detailed summary of the state of resources.
This booster works effectively in combination with Smart Balancer , as the collected data enables the Smart Balancer to make precise and thoughtful decisions, allowing the Master of Game to manage resources more effectively.
Compared to Researcher 30D , this booster lasts three times longer, enabling the Master of Game to focus on strategic planning without the need to purchase a new booster every month.
