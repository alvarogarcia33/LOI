## Connect+ 90D
Booster Connect+ 90D
Type: temporary
Duration: 90 days
A special tool designed to improve the Advisor's parameters in the Legends of Interactions game universe .
It allows the Master of Game to communicate with players who have interacted with any orbs on his/her plots of land , thus enabling the formation of mutually beneficial relationships.
Naturally, when communicating, one should adhere to ethical standards and avoid using expressions that could somehow offend or hurt the interlocutor.
