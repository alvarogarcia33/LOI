### Description of the object 
Orb ORB014052
Category: BILLBOARD
A medium-power orb from the BILLBOARD category. Like all objects in this category, it can share not only resources with players, but also valuable information by displaying a billboard with text. Such orbs are best suited for the Master of Game, who wants to make the most of the opportunities of earning income in the Legends of Interactions .
It is the Master of Game who decides what the orb will tell the player. This could be an advertisement for a business or event in the real world, or an invitation to a community on social media, or some important information related to the game. It all depends on your goals and strategies, of which there can be many.
This orb differs from a similar orb with basic properties in its accelerated recharge for further interaction. It is as much as 12 times faster! Thanks to this, the orb encourages the player to stay in place and interact with it every 5 minutes (12 times per hour), reading a message from the Master of Game.
An orb from this category that has almost reached its interaction limit can be charged with a special battery .
