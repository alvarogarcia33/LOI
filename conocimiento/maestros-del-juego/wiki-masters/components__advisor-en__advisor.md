## Advisor
ADVISOR
Advisor is the digital assistant of the Master of Game, which is equipped with artificial intelligence. It provides the Master of Game with information support and helps choose the optimal land management strategy in the world of Legends of Interactions .
The Advisor is indispensable in cases when the Master of Game owns a large number of plots of land that are difficult to keep track of on his/her own. The digital assistant analyzes the situation and, based on the collected information, gives advice on buying, developing and selling land. However, it is up to the Master of Game to make the final decision.
The Advisor has access to databases of geographic objects with the highest interaction potential. These may include UNESCO World Heritage sites , densely populated urban districts, etc. This information will be useful to the Master of Game when choosing plots of land.
In addition, the Advisor can learn and use the accumulated experience to improve the quality of the Advisor’s work, adapting to the requirements of a particular Master of Game.
The Advisor with basic capabilities displays:
· all plots under his/her management;
· orbs placed on these plots and their indicators;
· player activity on these plots.
The Advisor can also:
1. report whether there are any vacant plots within the territory under the Advisor’s control that are classified as UNESCO World Heritage Sites;
2. provide information on population density in the controlled territories;
3. show publicly accessible objects with high interaction potential: cafes, restaurants, etc.;
4. recommend the optimal number of orbs to place in a specific area;
5. recommend plots for purchase based on the ratio of frequently visited objects and the population density;
6. report when new plots of land go up for sale within the controlled area;
7. report on incoming offers for the purchase of plots of land.
The Advisor can manage a limited number of plots of land within a certain geographic proximity — a territory with a diameter of about 2 km. If the Master of Game buys a plot at a greater distance from the existing land holdings, then another Advisor is required.
A beginner Master of Game immediately receives a certain number of free Advisors. Additional Advisors can be purchased for HEXA Coin if necessary. The first Advisor received by the Master is the Chief Advisor . The Chief Advisor simplifies and systematizes work with other digital assistants.
Each plot of game land must be assigned to an Advisor. If there are no Advisors available, purchasing new plots becomes impossible.
By default, the Advisor is able to manage 127 plots of land. However, this number can be increased by using special tools — boosters . With the help of various boosters, you can also improve other parameters of the Advisor, making the Advisor’s work more efficient.
