### Description of the object 
Orb ORB034002
Category: standard
A perfectly balanced orb, which combines a rather powerful energy charge with an affordable cost. It is capable of providing twice as many resources as a basic orb from the same category. It is less common than MOGXXX001 .
This orb ranks second in the number of resources given to the player and the Master of Game as a result of interaction. At the same time, its recharge time for further interaction is the same as that of a standard orb with basic properties.
