## Aluminum
One of the crucial resources in the game. Aluminum in Legends of Interactions is a metal of perfect purity, the existence of which in the real world is impossible. This unique substance is formed in the orb as a result of mysterious processes that modern science cannot comprehend.
The resource is necessary for crafting certain game items and can be used in all sorts of game mechanics. The player can get some amount of aluminum by interacting with an orb of the corresponding type.
Aluminum in the real world combines lightness and amazing durability. Until people learned to produce this metal industrially, its value was higher than that of gold. The valuable qualities of aluminum are revealed in the game world in their entirety.
Aluminum as a resource expands the horizons of opportunities and the variety of game scenarios for players. It can become the key to success in many aspects of the gameplay. Accumulating aluminum reserves is a necessary part of any successful game strategy for anyone who strives for greatness in the world of Legends of Interactions .
