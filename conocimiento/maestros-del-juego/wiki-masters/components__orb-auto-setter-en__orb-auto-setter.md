## Orb Auto-Setter
Booster Orb Auto-Setter
Type: trainable
Training duration: 3 days
Requires: Orb Commander
A special tool designed to improve the Advisor's parameters in the Legends of Interactions game universe .
It allows the Advisor to independently place orbs on plots without any command from the Master of Game. Automatic placement is available only for standard orbs that do not have advertising options.
Orb Auto-Setter helps save time and quickly fill your game territory with objects.
The Advisor with this booster monitors the state of the land under his/her control and fills available plots with orbs. If a plot is freed from an orb, the Advisor immediately places a new one on it.
This booster can be activated and deactivated repeatedly.
