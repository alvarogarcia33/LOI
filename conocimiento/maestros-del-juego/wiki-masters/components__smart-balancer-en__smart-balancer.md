## Smart Balancer
Booster Smart Balancer
Type: trainable
Training duration: 3 days
Requires: Orb Commander
A special tool designed to improve the Advisor's parameters in the Legends of Interactions game universe .
With this booster, the Advisor evenly distributes resources across the plots of land of the Master of Game, replenishing the lack of certain resources on certain territories.
In this way, the Advisor ensures that all plots have the resources needed by the players.
This booster can interact with Orb Master , Researcher 30D , and Researcher 90D boosters.
