## Lithium
One of the valuable resources of Legends of Interactions . In the game universe, lithium is a unique metal with exceptional properties, formed in the orb as a result of the action of mysterious and enigmatic electromagnetic forces.
In the real world, this metal is actively used in nuclear reactors, electronics and pyrotechnics. These important and valuable qualities of lithium are reflected in the world of Legends of Interactions . This resource can be used both in all sorts of game mechanics and for crafting various items. The player can get certain amount of lithium when interacting with the orb of the appropriate type.
Thanks to this resource, players will be able to implement the most daring strategies and creative approaches, creating impressive game moments. Possession of lithium can give the player amazing prospects for expanding the game features, opening the way to new game mechanics and interactions.
With a properly developed strategy, it can become a powerful tool for achieving unprecedented heights in the Game and getting an incredibly emotional game experience.
