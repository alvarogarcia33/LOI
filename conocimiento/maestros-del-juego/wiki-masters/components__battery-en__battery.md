## Battery
ORB CHARGING BATTERY
An item that can be used by the Master of Game (MOG) when the orb has almost drained its energy charge and will soon stop interacting with players. The battery charges the orb, refilling it with energy for a certain number of interactions with the player.
The battery is available only for advertising orbs that are capable of displaying billboards and MAPS .
The possession of this item eliminates the need for the Master of Game to repurchase and reinstall the orb. It should be taken into account that orbs of some types may not be available for purchase at the required time. Additionally, placing new advertising orbs requires pre-moderation, which takes a certain amount of time.
The battery allows you to save time and continue using an orb that is in a favorable place and is regularly used by players for interaction. When using the battery, the orb receives the number of interactions that the battery contains. The number of interactions that the orb can receive is unlimited and depends only on the goals of the Master of Game and whether the MOG has the necessary batteries.
Each orb requires a battery of the appropriate type, depending on the resource produced.
A battery can be used only once.
