## Orb Master
Booster Orb Master
Type: trainable
Training duration: 3 days
Requires: Orb Commander
A special tool designed to improve the Advisor's parameters in the Legends of Interactions game universe .
It allows the Advisor to independently place orbs , while acting with greater thought and precision. Automatic placement is available only for standard orbs that do not have advertising options.
This booster helps save time and quickly fill your game territory with objects.
With this booster, the Advisor starts placing orbs from the center of the territory under his/her control.
The Master of Game can configure the booster’s settings:
· specify the number, types and colors of orbs for placement;
· mark plots where orbs should not be placed.
This booster can interact with the Smart Balancer booster.
