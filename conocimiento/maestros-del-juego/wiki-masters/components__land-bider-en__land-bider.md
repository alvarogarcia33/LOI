## Land Bider
Booster Land Bider
Type: standard
A special tool designed to improve the Advisor's parameters in the Legends of Interactions game universe .
It allows the Master of Game to place an offer for purchasing plots of game land owned by other Masters of Game.
Desired plots, owned by another Master of Game, are highlighted on the map, and a price is proposed for them. This is how a purchase offer is formed.
