### Description of the object 
Orb ORB013053
Category: BILLBOARD
The most powerful orb from the BILLBOARD category. Like all objects in this category, it has energy that can transmit information from the real world to the game universe. When interacting with it, the player receives not only resources , but also sees a billboard with an important message.
Such orbs are best suited for the Master of Game (MOG) who wants to make the most of the opportunities of earning income in the Legends of Interactions . With their help, the MOG can organize an advertising business in the Game universe and notify players about various goods or services in the real world. And this is only one of the many available strategies!
The key advantage of this particular orb is its lightning-fast recharge speed. Just 1 minute — and the object is ready for a new interaction! No other orb can match this phenomenal speed. It is ready to interact with the player every second minute, reporting information from the Master of Game over and over again. This orb, like a magnet, keeps the player close, causing a desire to continuously receive resources. Thus, it is a unique tool in its kind in terms of the intensity of the delivery of information beneficial to the Master of Game.
An orb from this category that has almost reached its interaction limit can be charged with a special battery .
