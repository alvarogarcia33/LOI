## Booster
BOOSTER FOR ADVISOR
A special tool used by the Master of Game in the Legends of Interactions game universe . It is intended to improve the Advisor .
There are many boosters, each of which changes one or more parameters of the Advisor. For example, it increases the number of plots that the digital assistant can control, or it allows the Advisor to independently place orbs on the game land.
The booster is available only in the Master of Game back office. It cannot be moved to the blockchain or transferred to another Master of Game.
A booster is activated after it is placed in one of the six slots next to the Advisor. Some boosters can be placed in any slot, while others are only intended for certain slots.
Each booster can be placed in a slot only once. If another booster is placed in an already occupied slot, the previous booster disappears and stops affecting the Advisor’s parameters.
All boosters can be divided into three types:
1. Standard. When activated, they change certain parameters of the Advisor once.
2. Temporary. They affect the Advisor’s parameters for some time, and disappear after the expiration of the term. Such boosters can be placed in several slots at once.
3. Trainable. They are installed for a certain period of time, during which they consistently change the parameters of the Advisor. After the booster disappears, the progress achieved by it is retained, and the Advisor’s parameters remain improved.
Boosters of all types can be purchased in the STORE section.
