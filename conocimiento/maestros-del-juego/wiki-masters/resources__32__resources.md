## Titanium
One of the valuable and sought-after game resources . Titanium in Legends of Interactions is a metal of perfect purity, surpassing the real one in its valuable properties. This special substance is formed in the orb as a result of mysterious processes that the human mind cannot comprehend.
The resource can be used both in all sorts of game mechanics and for crafting various items. The player obtains a certain amount of titanium when interacting with the orb of the corresponding type.
Titanium in the world of Legends of Interactions not only expands the functionality and flexibility of the gameplay, but also opens up new horizons for creativity and building successful strategies. Players who are able to use this metal as effectively as possible will ensure a sustainable advantage in the world of the Game.
