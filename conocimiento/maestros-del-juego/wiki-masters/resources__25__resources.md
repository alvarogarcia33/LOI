## Gallium
One of the most valuable and sought-after resources in Legends of Interactions . The substance is formed in the orb as a result of mysterious processes that are beyond the understanding of human mind.
In the real world, it is a soft and brittle metal that melts in the palm of your hand like chocolate. It is found only in the form of microscopic inclusions in host rocks. But according to many scientists, this amazing substance has a great future. In the world of Legends of Interactions , the bold predictions of scientists come true and gallium becomes an important and widely used resource.
The resource can be used in all sorts of game mechanics and for crafting various items. The player receives a certain amount of gallium when interacting with an orb of the appropriate type. Gallium opens up additional prospects for successful development for players.
By using its potential wisely, you can radically transform the gameplay, get a unique and exciting game experience. Those players who ensure themselves a reliable supply of this resource in advance will lay a solid foundation for leadership and prosperity in the game world.
