## Gold
One of the most valuable and sought-after resources . Gold in Legends of Interaction is a metal of perfect purity, the existence of which is impossible in the real world. This special substance is formed in the orb as a result of mysterious processes that modern science cannot comprehend.
In the Legends of Interaction universe, possessing gold is a source of the broadest opportunities. In the game world, the potential of the precious metal is revealed in its entirety. And those who have flexible strategic thinking can use gold to reach the heights of success and enjoy the exulting feeling of might.
The player can get some amount of gold when interacting with the orb of the corresponding type. Gold can be used in various game mechanics and for crafting individual items from it.
