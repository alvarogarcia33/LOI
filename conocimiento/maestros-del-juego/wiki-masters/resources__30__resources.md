## Silicium
Silicium in Legends of Interactions is a substance of absolute purity, the existence of which in the real world is impossible. This unique semi-metal is formed in the orb as a result of inscrutable processes that still remain a mystery to scientists.
In the real world, silicon is necessary for making high-tech devices. Likewise, in the Legends of Interactions universe, this resource will be needed by those who plan to craft powerful game items.
The potential of this resource in the game world is difficult to overestimate. This resource will provide the player with competitiveness and will allow him/her to quickly adapt to dynamically changing situation. With a correctly developed strategy, the supply of silicium will become an important factor in achieving success and prosperity in the Game. By correctly using the advantages of silicium, the player will increase his/her chances of achieving leadership positions in the world of Legends of Interactions .
The player can obtain a certain amount of silicium when interacting with an orb of the appropriate type. The resource can be used in all sorts of game mechanics and for crafting various items.
