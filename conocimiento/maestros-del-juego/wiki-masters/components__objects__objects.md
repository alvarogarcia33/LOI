## Objects
Game object — an interactive element that helps participants in the gameplay solve various tasks, develop and explore the limitless possibilities of the Legends of Interactions universe .
Objects come in different forms, each with its own unique set of qualities and functions. Some are completely mysterious and inexplicable. Others are embodied in the form of complex technical devices. There are even objects that possess intelligence and the ability to learn.
Certain objects are intended for players, while others — for the Masters of Game. However, some are useful to both at the same time.
All objects have one thing in common: they are intended for interaction.
The key objects in the Legends of Interactions world are orbs . By interacting with them, the player receives valuable game resources . Only the Masters of Game have the exclusive right to place orbs in the game space.
Players and Masters of Game can create some objects themselves by spending a certain amount of resources.
