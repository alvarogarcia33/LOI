## Researcher 30D
Booster Researcher 30D
Type: temporary
Duration: 30 days
A special tool designed to improve the Advisor's parameters in the Legends of Interactions game universe .
It allows the Advisor to monitor resources placed on all plots of land within the territory accessible to the Advisor.
Once per day, the Advisor provides a detailed summary of resources with this booster , both on his/her own plots and on the plots of other Masters of Game.
This enables the following:
· tracking the current state of resources on their own territories;
· monitoring the presence and movement of resources on lands of other Masters of Game.
This booster works effectively in combination with Smart Balancer , as the data gathered can help the Smart Balancer make optimal decisions, giving the Master of Game a competitive edge in resource management.
