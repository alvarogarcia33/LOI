## UNESCO
Land plots near UNESCO heritage sites
UNESCO World Heritage Sites are universally recognized treasures of mankind. They attract attention of millions of people. Land plots located near such sites can open up significant opportunities for player interactions.
Furthermore, for Masters of Game, owning these plots gives prestige and the chance to own unique game territory with some of the greatest cultural and historical landmarks.
Just imagine: You are the owner of land plots located near world-renowned sites such as the Great Wall of China, the Taj Mahal, or the famous Egyptian Pyramids!
Why is it beneficial for Masters of Game to acquire such land plots?
· interaction potential;
A land plot near a UNESCO Heritage site is highly likely to become a center of attraction for players, creating more opportunities for interaction and increasing potential profits.
· collectible value;
Owning a collection of land plots near UNESCO Heritage sites means holding a unique asset highly valued not only by players but also by other Masters of Game.
· long-term benefit.
These plots have a high potential for price growth.
Thus, owning land near UNESCO Heritage sites allows Masters of Game to combine several game strategies for land management.
The Master of Game can be assisted in choosing suitable sites from the UNESCO heritage list by a digital assistant, the Advisor .
