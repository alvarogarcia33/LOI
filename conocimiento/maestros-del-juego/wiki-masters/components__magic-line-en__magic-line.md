## Magic Line
Booster Magic Line
Type: standard
Requires: Orb Commander
A special tool designed to improve the Advisor's parameters in the Legends of Interactions game universe .
With this booster, the Master of Game can automatically place orbs on the game map. The Master of Game simply needs to draw a curved or straight line across the desired plots.
The width of the line can be adjusted, and the type of orbs to be placed can also be selected.
Example: A pedestrian road runs through the plots of the Master of Game, with large crowds walking along it daily. The Master wants orbs to be placed directly along this road, where potential players will pass. By activating the booster, the Master draws a line along the plots. The line coincides with the road, while the Advisor places orbs strictly along this line.
