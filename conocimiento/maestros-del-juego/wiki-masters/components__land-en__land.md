## Land
LAND
Land is the basis of the Legends of Interactions universe , the space where the diverse and exciting game world is formed. Without land, the existence and development of the Game is simply impossible.
The Game world is based on our planet. The entire globe is divided into land plots — hexagons with an area of ​​about 259 square meters. And since there are no identical places in the world, each plot is unique.
No matter where the player is on the Earth, he/she can be sure: this is part of the game space in which interaction is possible.
Each plot of land can potentially contain game objects and be an active part of the game world. Exploring new lands, the player discovers more and more opportunities in the Legends of Interactions universe.
For Masters of Game, land is the basis of business.
You buy land plots in Legends of Interactions and fill them with special objects. Players can collect and consume these resources , bringing you profit.
The success of the Master of Game depends on the value of the game land plots that he/she has. Different plots have different interaction potential. It is especially profitable to have land in places that are frequently visited by people: near famous landmarks, large shopping centers, amusement parks, etc.
However, even plots in less popular locations can become the basis for a successful project. Everything depends on the development strategy chosen by the Master of Game.
Possible strategies for working with land:
· obtaining unique game resources and their subsequent sale on the blockchain;
You can purchase plots of land and place game objects on them that players will interact with. This interaction will bring you resources.
The Master of Game can monetize the received resources through the blockchain: either on the Marketplace or by selling them directly to players.
· obtaining game resources in order to reward players for their actions that are beneficial to you;
Game resources can not only be monetized, but also used in other ways. For example, as payment to players for their actions that are beneficial to you. This may be, in particular, the promotion of your pages on social networks: subscriptions, reactions, sharing, etc.
This strategy can also be used to effectively attract new participants to the Game.
· purchasing and using game land for advertising and promoting business in the physical world;
We are talking here about advertising venues or events in the real world. There are different ways to use this strategy. For example, you can build chains of objects leading to a certain point in the game space.
· reselling plots;
You can buy land near a well-known and popular object, and then resell it at a good price. You can also buy a plot “with a perspective”, develop it, and then resell it at a higher price.
· collecting plots.
As we have already noted, some plots located in top world locations are very valuable. And you can buy them not only to fill them with special game objects or resell them, but also to create a personal collection.
Within this strategy, it is not the number of plots that is important, but their uniqueness.
For example, you can assemble a collection of plots located near UNESCO heritage sites ! Such a collection will definitely attract the attention of players.
The proposed strategies are advisory in nature. Their goal is to help you develop the Game and build an effective business in it. The strategies provided can be combined and adjusted depending on the progress of the Game.
All the necessary tools for managing plots are provided in the Masters of Game back office. Also, a special tool — the Advisor — has been developed for Masters of Game to help with effective land management.
