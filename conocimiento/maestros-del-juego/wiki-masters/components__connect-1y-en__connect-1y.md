## Connect+ 1Y
Booster Connect+ 1Y
Type: temporary
Duration: 1 year
A special tool designed to improve the Advisor's parameters in the Legends of Interactions game universe .
It allows the Master of Game to communicate with players who have interacted with any orbs on his/her plots of land for a whole year. Otherwise, it operates on the same principle as Connect+ 90D .
