## Maps
MAPS
A special option provided by orbs from the MAPS category. This option can be used primarily for advertising purposes.
MAPS contains text information prepared for the player by the Master of Game. In this regard, the player does not necessarily need to interact with the orb to see this information. It is enough to tap on the orb icon on the game map displayed by the scanner to read the text. This is the key difference between MAPS and a billboard .
MAPS can contain any information: from an advertisement of a local cafe to an invitation to a concert. The maximum text size: 63 characters with spaces. In this way, the Master of Game can carry out advertising business in the Legends of Interactions universe .
