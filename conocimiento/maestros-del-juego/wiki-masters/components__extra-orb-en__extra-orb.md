## Extra Orb
Booster Extra Orb
Type: temporary
Duration: 30 days
A special tool designed to improve the Advisor's parameters in the Legends of Interactions game universe .
It allows the Masters of Game to place 1 additional orb on each of their plots of land .
An Advisor with basic capabilities can place only 1 orb per assigned plot. Therefore, when using Extra Orb, this number doubles.
Such boosters can be placed in multiple slots, further increasing the number of orbs available for placement several times.
Example:
The Master of Game (MOG) installs Extra Orb boosters in three slots. Now, he/she can place 4 orbs on each of his/her plots of land.
This makes it possible to attract more players and significantly enhance interaction potential within the MOG’s territory.
