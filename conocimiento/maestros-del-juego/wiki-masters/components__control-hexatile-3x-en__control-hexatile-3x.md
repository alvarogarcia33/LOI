## Control Hexatile 3x
Booster Control Hexatile 3x
Type: standard
A special tool designed to improve the Advisor's parameters in the Legends of Interactions game universe .
It allows to increase the number of plots of land that the Advisor can manage.
It expands the Advisor's zone of geographical influence to 381 plots, which is three times the base capacity of the digital assistant.
