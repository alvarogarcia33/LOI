### Description of the object 
Orb ORB013001
Category: standard
A basic orb from the standard category. Standard orbs are capable of only one type of interaction — giving resources to the player and the Master of Game (MOG).
This object has a weak energy charge and produces the lowest amount of resources in its category. Its main advantages — low cost and high availability — make it an excellent choice for the beginner Masters of Game. You can start exploring the game world and its mechanics, purchase an impressive number of such orbs and fill a large area of ​​the game space with them without spending a lot of funds. If you wish, this can become one of your strategies in the Legends of Interactions universe .
