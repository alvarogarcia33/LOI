## Shard Setter 30D
Booster Shard Setter 30D
Type: temporary
Duration: 30 days
A special tool designed to improve the Advisor's parameters in the Legends of Interactions game universe .
It allows the Master of Game to place special objects — shards — on his/her plots. Fragments of shards can be found and collected by players. The found fragments can then be combined by players into symbols (pages).
Placing shards increases the attractiveness of plots for players. The more diverse objects the Master of Game places on his/her territory, the longer players will stay on his/her plots instead of moving to other areas of the game world.
