## Coal
One of the important resources in Legends of Interactions . This substance has unique properties and is formed in the orb as a result of processes that are currently beyond human comprehension.
It can be used in all sorts of game mechanics and for crafting various items. To obtain coal, the player must find the corresponding orb and interact with it. The amount of the resource obtained depends on the type of orb .
In the real world, coal has been a valuable resource since ancient times, necessary for smelting copper. It also played a key role in the industrial revolution, providing energy for steam engines and factories.
In the world of Legends of Interactions , coal is also an important and valuable resource. Possession of coal is not just a necessary component for sustainable development in the game world. By investing in it, the player lays the foundation for long-term success and triumphant prosperity. This resource is a trusted companion on the exciting path of victories and achievements in the game.
