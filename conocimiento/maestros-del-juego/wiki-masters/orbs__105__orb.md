### Description of the object 
Orb ORB012071
Category: MAPS
MAPS orbs, like BILLBOARD orbs, also share resources with players and transmit information from the real world to the game world. However, unlike BILLBOARD orbs, these orbs are capable of displaying information on the game map.
As a result, the player sees a message from the Master of Game in the form of MAPS from afar, without even interacting with the orb. The player just needs to click on the icon on the map to read the message, and if there are several players in a given location, then they can simultaneously receive the message transmitted by the orb.
In this sense, the MAPS orb is like a billboard that attracts attention. This opens up excellent business opportunities for the Master of Game. By placing such orbs over a large area, the Master of Game increases the likelihood that a large number of players will see the advertisement, and, therefore, can count on more favorable terms of cooperation with the advertiser.
An orb from this category that has almost reached its interaction limit can be charged with a special battery .
