### Description of the object 
Orb ORB021051
Category: BILLBOARD
A basic orb from the BILLBOARD category. A fairly rare object in the game world. Objects of this category have a special energy that serves as a conductor of information from the real world to the game universe. This orb can not only provide resources , but also inform the player of important information by showing a billboard with text.
The Master of Game can use this property of the orb to realize various goals and strategies, for example, to place some messages or advertisements. Having agreed on cooperation with the owner of a business in the real world, the Master of Game can place an orb with a billboard on his/her plot. By interacting with the orb, the player will not only get resources, but will also see an advertisement, which will benefit the business owner. In turn, the Master of Game will benefit from the concluded deal.
Certainly, a talking orb can also be used for self-promotion, for example, to attract the public to your blog or YouTube channel. There can be many options. It all depends on the goals and desires of the Master of Game.
An orb from this category that has almost reached its interaction limit can be charged with a special battery .
