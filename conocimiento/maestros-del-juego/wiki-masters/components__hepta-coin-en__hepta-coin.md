## HEPTA Coin
Main coin in the Legends of Interactions economy. It is an everyday currency used by players to purchase basic game items and resources . In fact, some of these items can only be bought with HEPTA Coin.
Therefore, HEPTA Coin is an asset essential for any player. Without this coin, success in the Game is simply impossible. As a result, there is always a steady demand from players for HEPTA Coin.
Players obtain HEPTA Coin as follows:
· About 10% of the coins are awarded for achievements in the Game.
· The remaining 90% can be mined using a special game item called the HEPTAMINER.
Like other game coins, HEPTA Coin is tokenized on the NETSBO blockchain. This allows it to be traded on the Antallagí.io Marketplace, thereby enabling players to monetize their experience of interactions with the Game.
