## Orb
Orb — a key game object that players interact with to obtain resources in the Legends of Interactions universe .
The origin of the orbs is unknown, which adds even more mystery to their already unique nature. The object is a radiant sphere filled with swirling energy vortexes — so powerful that they are capable of generating matter. Within this sparkling force field, resources, which have a wide range of applications in the game world, are generated.
The right to place orbs on plots of land is reserved exclusively for the Masters of Game who own those plots.
By successfully interacting with the orb through a device with augmented reality (AR) functionality, the player receives a certain amount of resources. At the same time, the Master of Game who placed the orb also receives some of the resources. In addition, the Master of Game receives the most valuable coin in the world of the Game — OCTA Coin . The Master of Game can later monetize the obtained resources and coins on the Antallagí.io Marketplace.
Among other things, the exceptional properties of some orbs allow the Master of Game to use them to organize an advertising business in the game world.
The orb is intended for a certain number of interactions. After each interaction, it requires some time to recharge. Depending on the type of orb, the recharge time can range from 1 minute to 24 hours. Once the orb recharges, the player can interact with it again.
When the interaction limit is exhausted, the orb disappears. An orb that has not yet fully exhausted its interaction limit can be recharged using a special battery (battery is available only for orbs of certain categories).
In the first version of the Legends of Interactions universe, the orb became one of the fundamental game objects. Orbs come in different types, differing in properties and functionality.
In general, orbs are divided into 4 groups, each of which has its own characteristics:
· the blue group includes regular orbs, which give the player one unit of the resource;
· the green group unites regular orbs that can give the player two units of the resource;
· the red group contains regular orbs that give the player five units of the resource;
· the NEON group includes all advertising orbs.
In addition, orbs are classified according to the type of resources produced. There are currently 12 types:
Gold, Aluminum, Coal, Gallium, Iron, Limestone, Lithium, Crude oil, Silicium, Silver, Titanium and Copper.
