## Control Hexatile 27x
Booster Control Hexatile 27x
Type: standard
A special tool designed to improve the Advisor's parameters in the Legends of Interactions game universe .
It allows to increase the number of plots of land that the Advisor can manage.
It expands the Advisor's zone of geographical influence to 3,429 plots, which is 27 times the base capacity of the digital assistant.
