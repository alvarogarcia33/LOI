## OCTA Coin
The most valuable game coin — a kind of premium currency in Legends of Interactions . It allows players to buy the most expensive and exclusive items in the Game.
The Master of Game gets OCTA Coin at the moment a player obtains a resource from an orb placed by that Master.
Players earn OCTA Coin as a reward for their achievements in the Game.
However, the share of coins awarded to players is minimal. 90% of all OCTA Coins are in the hands of Masters of Game.
Because the coin is tokenized on the NETSBO blockchain, it can also be used outside the Game. Specifically, it can be offered for sale on the Antallagí.io Marketplace, where players and other Masters of Game come to buy the resources they need.
Thus, OCTA Coin owners possess a highly valuable asset that can be monetized if they wish so.
