## Land Buyer
Land Buyer Booster
Type: standard
A special tool designed to improve the Advisor's parameters in the Legends of Interactions game universe .
The Advisor with this booster automatically buys all the plots of land that were put up for sale by other Masters of Game in the area available to the Advisor.
To use this booster, just click on the desired plot. After that, the Advisor will start buying all the plots put up for sale located around the selected point.
The buying process will continue without stopping — as long as the Master of Game has enough REEX Coin to purchase the land.
