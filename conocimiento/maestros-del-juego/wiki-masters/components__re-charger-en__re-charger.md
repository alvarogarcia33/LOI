## Re-charger
Booster Re-charger
Type: trainable
Training duration: 3 days
A special tool designed to improve the Advisor's parameters in the Legends of Interactions game universe .
With this booster, the Advisor recharges the Master’s of Game advertising orbs , such as BILLBOARD and MAPS.
The recharging process is random. The Advisor will recharge all orbs that are close to running out of interaction limits. All available batteries are used for this.
