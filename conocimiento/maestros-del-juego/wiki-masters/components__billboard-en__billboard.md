## Billboard
BILLBOARD
A special option provided by orbs from the BILLBOARD category. As the name suggests, this option can be used primarily for advertising purposes.
When a player interacts with the orb, a banner appears that contains text information prepared by the Master of Game. The maximum text size: 63 characters with spaces. This can be anything from an advertisement of a local cafe to an invitation to a concert. In this way, the Master of Game can carry out advertising business in the Legends of Interactions universe .
In addition to the billboard, there is also the MAPS option.
