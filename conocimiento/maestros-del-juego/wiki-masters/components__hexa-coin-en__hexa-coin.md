## HEXA Coin
HEXA Coin is a special coin designed for purchasing land plots within the Game’s ecosystem. Its use is intended only for Masters of Game.
On the purchased plots of game land, Masters of Game can place special objects — orbs . Later, players will interact with these objects to obtain valuable resources . Thus, game land serves as the foundation of business of the Master of Game.
Because the coin is tokenized on the NETSBO blockchain, it can also be used outside the Game’s ecosystem. In particular, it can be offered for sale on the Antallagí.io Marketplace, where players and other Masters of Game come to buy the resources they need.
HEXA Coin can be purchased on the Antallagí.io Marketplace.
