## Crude oil
One of the most valuable and significant resources in Legends of Interactions . In the world of the Game, this is a unique substance with special properties that is born in the orb as a result of physical processes inaccessible to modern science.
Possession of this resource will give the player a powerful incentive for growth and development in Legends of Interactions . Its competent use will help to significantly improve the game dynamics, increase the excitement of the gameplay, implement the most daring strategies and creative approaches. By betting on oil, the player lays a reliable foundation for his/her long-term and sustainable success in Legends of Interactions .
This resource can be used both in all sorts of game mechanics and for crafting various items. The player can get a certain amount of oil when interacting with an orb of the corresponding type.
