## Shards
Shards — a type of objects placed in the game world by the game system. Players can collect shards and use them for various actions, including obtaining in-game items.
Each shard is divided into 9 fragments, which are scattered in a random order.
To place shards on his/her plots, the Master of Game will need one of the following boosters : Shard Setter 30D or Shard Setter 90D .
