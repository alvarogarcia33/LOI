## Limestone
One of the resources in Legends of Interactions . This special material is formed in the orb as a result of mysterious processes that modern science cannot comprehend.
The player can obtain certain amount of limestone by interacting with an orb of the corresponding type. The resource is necessary for crafting certain items in the Game, and can also be used in various game mechanics.
In the real world, it is quite ordinary and widespread material. But in the fantasy world of Legends of Interactions , this resource has significant potential that can transform the gameplay.
Its skillful and rational use will not only increase the excitement of your game, but will take it to a completely different level, setting new boundaries of the possible, leading to the very pinnacle of success.
