## BaitMaster
Booster BaitMaster
Type: trainable
Training duration: 2 days
A special tool designed to improve the Advisor's parameters in the Legends of Interactions game universe .
The Game’s system, without human intervention, distributes in-game objects and items across the territories of players’ habitation based on special algorithms. These objects and items can attract players’ attention and assist them in achieving their goals in the Game. These can be coins, elements of game inventory, etc.
BaitMaster increases the probability that the system will place items that players are interested in on the game plots of land of the Master of Game, which are controlled by a specific Advisor. During the training of the Advisor, this probability grows, and the usage of one BaitMaster booster increases the chances of placement by 1% of the standard settings of the Game system.
By using BaitMaster boosters, it is possible to increase the probability of placing valuable objects on the plots under the Advisor's control by up to 10%.
Thus, the Master of Game can attract even more players to his/her plots by using BaitMaster.
Example:The Master of Game activated the BaitMaster booster. Over the two days of the booster’s operation, the probability that the system will place objects of interest to players on the plots of the Master of Game increased by 1%. After that, the Master of Game activated another BaitMaster, increasing this figure by another 1%. As a result, the probability of placing valuable objects on the plots of the Master of Game increased by 2% compared to the standard settings of the Game system.
