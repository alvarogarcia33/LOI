## Iron
One of the important resources in the Game. Iron in the Legends of Interactions world differs in its properties from ordinary iron and is not subject to any chemical or physical impacts. Iron in the game universe is a metal of absolute purity, which is unattainable in the real world. This is a unique substance that is formed in the orb as a result of mysterious processes that have not yet been understood by science.
Those who take care of ensuring a reliable supply of iron in the Legends of Interactions Game in advance will open up new spaces for game interactions. Reasonable and effective use of this resource will allow the player to successfully achieve their goals, making the game experience more interesting and multifaceted.
Iron can be used both in all sorts of game mechanics and for crafting various items. The player can obtain a certain amount of this resource when interacting with an orb of the appropriate type.
