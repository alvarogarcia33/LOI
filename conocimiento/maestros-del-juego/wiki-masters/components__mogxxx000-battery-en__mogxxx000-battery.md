## MOGXXX000 Battery
MOGXXX000 BATTERY
Suitable for recharging the following types of orbs :
BILLBOARD orbs MOGXXX053 , MOGXXX052 , MOGXXX051 .
MAPS orbs MOGXXX072 , MOGXXX071 .
When using this battery, the orb receives the number of interactions that the battery contains. The number of interactions that the orb can receive is unlimited and depends only on the goals of the Master of Game (MOG) and whether the MOG has the necessary batteries.
Each orb requires a battery of the appropriate type, depending on the resource produced.
The battery can be used only once.
