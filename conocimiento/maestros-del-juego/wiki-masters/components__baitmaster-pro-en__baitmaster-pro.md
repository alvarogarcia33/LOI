## BaitMaster Pro
Booster BaitMaster Pro
Type: trainable
Training duration: 4 days
A special tool designed to improve the Advisor's parameters in the Legends of Interactions game universe .
The Game’s system, without human intervention, distributes in-game objects and items across the territories of players’ habitation based on special algorithms. These objects and items can attract players’ attention and assist them in achieving their goals in the Game. These can be coins, elements of game inventory, etc.
BaitMaster Pro increases the probability that the Game system will place any items that players are interested in on the territory of a specific Advisor. It allows to continue increasing this indicator from the maximum of 10% achieved when using regular BaitMasters . Each BaitMaster Pro booster can increase the probability of placing objects by 1%. This figure can be increased to 20% probability of the standard settings of the Game system for the distribution of items and resources.
Thus, the Master of Game can attract even more players to his/her plots.
Example:
The system places objects of interest to players with a probability of 10% on the plots of the Master of Game. The Master of Game used five consecutive BaitMaster Pro boosters. Each of them increased this probability by 1%, and now the chances of placing these objects are increased by 15% compared to the standard settings of the Game system.
